Design reference for future C# implementation:

AppRoot = AppContext.BaseDirectory
ConfigDirectory = Path.Combine(AppRoot, "config")
DataDirectory = Path.Combine(AppRoot, "data")
DatabasePath = Path.Combine(DataDirectory, "database.db")
ReportsDirectory = Path.Combine(AppRoot, "reports")
ExportsDirectory = Path.Combine(AppRoot, "exports")
LogsDirectory = Path.Combine(AppRoot, "logs")
BackupsDirectory = Path.Combine(DataDirectory, "backups")

Never hard-code C:\Instageram or any machine-specific absolute path.
